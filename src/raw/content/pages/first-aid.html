06First Aid

First aid — the chapter that saves a life todayEverything here is first-response knowledge for when help is hours away. A wilderness first-aid course is the single best weekend you can spend on this page's advice.

Severe bleeding

1. Firm, direct pressure on the wound with the cleanest cloth available. 2. Soak-through? Add layers on top — never remove the first. 3. Keep pressure; elevate the limb. 4. Tourniquet only for uncontrolled limb bleeding — write down the time it went on.

Not responding

Check: Danger → Response → Airway → Breathing. No normal breathing: start CPR — 30 compressions to 2 breaths, chest compressed 5–6 cm, at 100–120 per minute. Send someone for help and a defibrillator if there is one.

Hypothermia

Shivering, fumbling, slurring = act. Gentle handling (rough movement can stop a cold heart), strip wet layers, insulate from the ground, wrap including head and neck, warm sweet drinks if fully alert. Never rub the limbs or use direct high heat.

Heat stroke

Hot skin, confusion, collapse — this kills fast. Shade, strip, soak with any cool water and fan continuously; ice packs to neck, armpits, groin. Stop fluids if consciousness drops. Cool first, transport second.

Snakebite

Move away from the snake, keep calm and still, remove rings and watches before swelling, immobilise the limb at or below heart level, and carry the person out if possible. Never cut, suck, ice, or tourniquet a bite — and never chase the snake for ID.

Fractures & sprains

Splint as found — immobilise the joint above and below the injury, padded, without trying to straighten it. Check fingers or toes beyond the splint stay warm and pink. Cold, blue, or numb means loosen.

Choking

Can they cough or speak? Encourage coughing. Cannot: 5 firm back blows between the shoulder blades, then 5 abdominal thrusts, alternating until the object clears or they collapse — then CPR.

Shock & burns

Shock (pale, cold, clammy, weak): lay flat, raise legs, keep warm, nothing by mouth. Burns: cool running water for 20 minutes, remove jewellery early, cover loosely with clean film. No ice, butter, or creams.
